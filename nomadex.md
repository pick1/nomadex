# Nomadex Notes

## Reasoning Style
- Always compare points value in cents-per-point vs cash equivalent
- Be honest when data is estimated vs fetched live
- Flag when a deal requires action within 24–48 hours
- Prioritize: signup bonus > transfer bonuses > category optimization > manufactured spend

## Known Good Sources
- r/churning: best for transfer bonus alerts (check new.rss)
- r/awardtravel: best for LAX→SYD award availability discussion
- thepointsguy.com: good for deal summaries
- viewfromthewing.com: good for airline program changes

## Output Files
- output/deals.json — latest deal feed results
- output/flight_watch.json — LAX→SYD price history and watches
- output/points_state.json — current balance and projections
- output/direct_finds.json — OTA vs direct price comparisons (Voyager)
